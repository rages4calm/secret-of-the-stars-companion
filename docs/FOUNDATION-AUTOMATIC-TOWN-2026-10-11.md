## Standard automatic generation

The pinned analyzer discovers code from authentic PC/M/X entry contexts. Its upstream decoder and IR emitter produce the private generated bodies; this release does not manually lower another title routine. The manifest contains 13 root-mode contexts and 41 discovered variants: 37 eligible and four interpreted. These are generation counts, not executed-function or campaign counts.

The generic patch commits original instruction fetch/data/stack bus transfers and internal clocks one instruction at a time. It corrects repeated indirect-pointer evaluation, native index-width truncation, actual long-return bank/frame handling and scheduler ownership on fallback. Unsupported instructions/modes and unproven control idioms keep original execution. Existing guarded clear and constructor islands remain enabled in player launches; strict automatic-only comparisons disable both separately.

Exact032 passes 166 controls across 14 copied runs: five original-byte route comparisons plus save/cold restore/rollback. Ordered cold samples match all 948,448 mixed original/generated states, with 52,418 actually generated steps across 209 PCs. The floor sample matches 12,824 states, with 746 generated steps across 125 PCs. An emitted snippet is not counted as execution merely because it exists. The original-control target list and counters are bound to the actual manifest.

Setting the native index-width flag truncates both index registers. [WDC W65C816S datasheet, section 2.7](https://www.westerndesigncenter.com/wdc/documentation/w65c816s.pdf).

## Rendering-reference decision and next work
