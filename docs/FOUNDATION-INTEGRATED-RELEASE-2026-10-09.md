## What changed

With experimental widescreen enabled, the observer can draw the authenticated
nonwrapping parked Algos components and the strictly selected later world-map
ship family in the margins. It follows original constructors, graphics and a
completed 544-byte OAM packet. Travel checks preserve the original body slots
and exact reserved shadow proxy. The proxy is never extended into side margins.
Wrapping B0/A8, unknown allocations, HDMA, changed resources and unqualified
packet/raster discontinuities retain their refusal paths. The original center,
guest state, activation, collision and CPU cycle budgets remain unchanged.

The journal can show `STARCREST: HELD NOW` when either original party inventory
currently contains it. It recomputes after item removal, cold restore and rewind;
it does not invent a completed quest or remember a future inventory after rollback.
The existing Journal setting and J / left-stick-click binding control visibility.
Advanced `JournalPossession=0` in foundation.ini disables this inventory line.
`SNESRECOMP_FOUNDATION_JOURNAL_POSSESSION=0|1` explicitly overrides that option;
the earlier experimental environment variable is not used in production.
The original opening lead remains available when the item is absent.

Widescreen still defaults off. Enhanced original pixels remain independently
selectable. Existing custom bindings, reward rates, movement boost and save bytes
are retained. No original artwork, audio or gameplay data is bundled.
