# Roadmap

The target is a polished, editable Windows PC edition with attractive original
pixels, true wider scenery/actors, reliable controls/audio and a useful journal.
This is active foundation work, not a completed native recompilation.

## 1. Public first-run setup and packaging

Companion now connects ROM selection/validation, source-build progress and
cancellation, isolated profile adoption, explicit compilation status, title-first
launch and selected copied-save resume. Public Play no longer requires private
fixture/checkpoint data. XP/gold rates, guarded movement boost and keyboard/
controller hotkeys are editable in the launcher.
Source-profile updates now create verified new folders and retain old profiles
for selection-only rollback. A source-first package tool includes one
self-contained GUI, authored source and notices; native game and original data
stay excluded. Finish broader package/hardware checks and automatic GUI release
updates. Configured profile roots currently retain absolute paths.
Do not distribute the ROM or generated game code.

## 2. Finish widescreen actors and difficult scenes

The [native0.2.4 bundle](docs/FOUNDATION-PRESENTATION-BUNDLE-2026-10-10.md)
authenticates selected overworld packet/raster/resource identity on both
horizontal and vertical axes in both directions. Original saved parked
vehicle allocations and original shadows widen under the same bounded
relation rather than two diagnostic coordinate tuples. Original-loader staged
checks cover eight allocations and eight directional seams; five allocations
show side art and three are valid no-side-art controls. Original travel proxies
remain in fixed center slots. Unknown/stale/unsupported contexts retain fallback.
Natural acquisition and full campaign traversal remain separate qualifications.

The [earlier0.2.1 foundation](docs/FOUNDATION-INTEGRATED-RELEASE-2026-10-09.md)
and [0.2.3 continuation](docs/FOUNDATION-CONTINUATION-2026-10-10.md) retain
their earlier installed guards and experimental scopes.

Extend coherent multi-party, reserved-slot, vehicle, effect and cutscene contexts.
Keep original activation/collision, exact center and fallback while each new
family qualifies. Broad terrain/OBJ overlap and subscreen/half-color contexts
remain incomplete. Resource enumeration does not certify a campaign.
The [staged assessment](docs/FOUNDATION-STAGED-PROGRESSION-2026-10-09.md)
retains the separate experimental provenance and original-loader recipes.
Natural Algos acquisition and later world-map story entry remain separate from
staged execution. Experimental build receipts stay refused by normal Setup.

## 3. Expand the quest journal

The integrated current-Starcrest-possession card reads both original inventories
and recomputes after restore. It is useful current-state evidence, while the
original award, completion event and full journal history still need separate
proof. Staged item fixtures are now authorized for these focused checks.

Native0.2.4 adds a full objective/route/current-progress panel and the original
Uncle Save stage-one Likado clue to David, Aris/Pennon and Bosen advice. All
reconcile after restore and rollback. [Current bundle](docs/FOUNDATION-PRESENTATION-BUNDLE-2026-10-10.md).
Add natural Crest acquisition and further dialogue/progression fixtures; verify actual item/story
writers before enabling completion rules. Define deterministic event history,
new-game reset, cold restore and rewind behavior. Avoid spoilers or an external
append-only log that remembers a future state after rollback.
The [Crest assessment](docs/FOUNDATION-JOURNAL-CREST-ASSESSMENT-2026-10-09.md)
establishes item identity and generic insertion/removal, but not the title-specific
award or durable completion event. Opening victories are not a Crest fixture.

An optional navigation map is a proposed companion to the journal. The original
game provides no built-in map screen ([player account](https://www.grouvee.com/games/384353-tecmo-secret-of-the-stars/)); its
[booklet](https://www.videogamemanual.com/snes/Tecmo%20Secret%20of%20the%20Stars%20%28USA%29.pdf) includes a printed world map. A new map should read owned game geometry,
show a verified player position, and distinguish visited locations from optional
spoiler information; controls must be assignable on keyboard and controller.

## 4. Audio and presentation polish

Keep enhanced original pixels and experimental widescreen moving toward a
consistent whole-game presentation. A replacement HD world/sprite pack is
deferred. Optional finer menu-frame art has only bounded coverage.

MSU-1 is conditional: first establish a compatible title driver, event/track
mapping, authorized local soundtrack input, loops/fades and original-audio
fallback. No verified compatible driver/pack is integrated today.

## 5. Wider testing

Use small copied-save and reproducible routes for story transitions, combat,
menus, controllers, listening checks, long sessions and hardware variation.
Protect the newest owner save and keep failures/coverage gaps in the evidence.
A full campaign, every actor/effect and every physical controller are still
unqualified. Volunteer micro-tests are welcome; another owner playthrough is
not the default requirement.

## Archived CPU experiments

The owner ended the CPU experiment work on October 10. Metatile and dispatch
results remain reference evidence, excluded from player builds. No further CPU
experiments are scheduled. Guest cycle accounting stays original; movement boost
and fast-forward remain explicit player options. Complete native/AOT execution
remains an unfinished architectural goal, not a claim about this release.
