Repeat while evidence supports progress; stop on completion, essential missing authority/input, or no measurable progress. The current continuation starts with the optional current-area map and its configurable keyboard/controller action, followed by the next original later-chapter journal clues and exact combined qualification. The overarching goal remains active until the finished project criteria are actually achieved.

Records follow the existing private `local/evidence/` convention; no private data is published. This loop does not create a timer, recurring job, or external-message authorization.
